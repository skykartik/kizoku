Why Kizoku

Most live wallpaper tools are heavy.

A browser engine, a runtime, a background service. Kizoku is one native Windows program that draws its own interface.

The usual way

  • Ships a whole browser engine or runtime
  • Background service that stays resident
  • Often wants an account
  • Keeps rendering behind your game

Kizoku

  • One native .exe, nothing to install around it
  • Uses the codecs already on your PC
  • No accounts, no telemetry
  • Pauses for games, full-screen apps and maximized windows
